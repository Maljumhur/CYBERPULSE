05 · FINAL INSIGHTS

Four Findings That Matter

INSIGHT 01
GOVERNMENT TARGETS
1,770
State institutions and political systems appeared in 1,770 documented incidents, the highest sector count.
INSIGHT 02
SYSTEM HIJACKING
1,800
Hijacking with Misuse was the most frequent attack category, appearing in 1,800 incidents.
INSIGHT 03
THE 2023 PEAK
723
The dataset recorded 723 incidents beginning in 2023, the highest annual count in the analyzed period.
INSIGHT 04
FREQUENCY ≠ INTENSITY
4.0
Ransomware showed relatively high median weighted intensity (about 4), even though it was less frequent than several other categories.
Findings represent documented EuRepoC incidents and do not directly measure global cyberattack prevalence or national cybersecurity risk.
